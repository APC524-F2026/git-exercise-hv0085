Part 2
I prefer Iris's fix. Her change makes legendre_polynomial return exact Fraction coefficients,
and her test compares them with exact equality. Frank's fix instead converts those same fractions
down to Decimal and compares within a tolerance — but once the underlying values are exact,
checking them approximately throws that exactness away: a small bug could fall inside the
tolerance and pass silently, where the exact comparison would catch it.
Frank's orthogonolity test checks the orthogonal property of the legendre_polynomials.
However, This test is independent of the choice of the coefficients tests.
